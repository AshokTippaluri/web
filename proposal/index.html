🌹 For My Love 🌹

🌅 In every sunrise, every gentle breeze,
❤️ Your presence brings my heart such perfect ease.
🏡 With you, each moment feels like coming home,
💞 So tell me, love, will you no longer roam?
🤝 Will you stay beside me, hand in loving hand,
🌟 And journey through forever in this promised land? 🌹
